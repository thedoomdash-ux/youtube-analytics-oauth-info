# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 22, 2026**

## Overview

Local Email Sender is a private, owner-operated desktop application. It is used only by the owner of the Gmail accounts that explicitly authorize it.

## Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the application may access Gmail message metadata and content needed to send scheduled messages, continue approved threads, detect relevant replies, inspect delivery-status notices, and maintain local sending safeguards.

The application does not request passwords, payment information, browsing history, or unrelated Google services.

## How data is used

Authorized Gmail data is used only to operate the owner's local email-sending and follow-up workflows. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

## Storage and security

Sender queues, message history, and operational state are stored on the owner's local computer. OAuth client credentials and refresh tokens are stored in the sender's local credential storage and are not committed to source control or public repositories.

## Sharing

The application does not disclose Gmail data to unrelated third parties. Google APIs are used to provide Gmail functionality. Any notification service configured separately by the owner receives only the notification information the owner chooses to send.

## Retention and deletion

The owner may disconnect an account or revoke access at any time through Google Account permissions. Local queues and history can be removed by the owner from the sender's data directory.
